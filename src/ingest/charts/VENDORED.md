# Vendored from tactician `tools/charts/` (WP10 phase 6, 2026-11-07)

The TCP1 chart builder was written in tactician for WP10 stage 1 (one local
English Channel pack) and moved here for stage 2, where it is cut into z6
cells and published to R2. Source: `deepregatta/tactician`, `tools/charts/`,
main at `eaf6d88` (2026-11-07). The format document moved with it
([docs/chart-pack-format.md](../../../docs/chart-pack-format.md)), and so did
the golden fixture (`tests/fixtures/chart-pack-cherbourg/`, byte-identical,
regenerated byte for byte by `scripts/make_chart_fixture.py`).

## Changed here since (phase 8, 2026-11-07)

- `shom.py`: the sector radii named (`ARC_NM`, `LEG_FACTOR`, `REACH_NM`); same output.
- `build.py`: a cell reads SHOM past its box by `REACH_NM` and 0.01°
  (`shom_bbox`); depth and sea distances come from the cell's EMODnet product
  (`products.py`, new) instead of the grid inside each tile's task; a target
  whose inputs equal its published entry's is not built (`unchanged_if`).
  The golden fixture still regenerates byte for byte.
- `depth.py`: blocks past the DTM's extent are no data (`covered`).
- `sources.py`: `newest_seamap`, one HEAD a day back, for the monthly run's
  `newest`.
- `common.py`: the `europe` cell set.

## What stayed in tactician, and why

- **The style**: `style.mjs`, `style_fields.mjs` (which derives
  `style-fields.json` from the style's pinned packages), `build_style.py`,
  `check_style.py`, `scan_packs.py`, `glyph_pbf.py`, `vendor/`,
  `style-usage.json`, `style-native-support.json` and its Node packages. The
  style ships inside the apps and is tactician's; the producer only needs the
  property list it derives, vendored here. When tactician's style reads a new
  property, it regenerates `style-fields.json` there, and this copy is
  vendored again with its new digest (a rebuild of every cell follows, since
  the file is part of the recipe).
- **`mvt.py`** also stays there (its `scan_packs.py` reads packs with it).
- **`measure.py`** and `build.py --measure`: they priced stage 1's mask, road
  rule and depth packaging; the phase 1 tables are dated evidence in
  tactician's offline charts plan, reproducible from tactician at eaf6d88.
